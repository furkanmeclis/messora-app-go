The name or legal name, Turkish national identity number or tax office and number, billing address, telephone number and the e-mail address registered in the account, as declared by the Buyer during ordering.

## 3. Essential characteristics of the Service

- **Service:** {{marka}} — team inbox, bulk campaign, template builder, automation, AI agent, analytics and reporting software running on the official WhatsApp Business Cloud API. It is provided over the internet as software as a service (SaaS).
- **Plan and period:** The plan and the monthly or yearly subscription period stated in the order summary. Plan contents and limits (WhatsApp numbers, users, AI agents, active automations, monthly AI credits, API quota) are published on the [Pricing](/en/pricing) page.
- **Minimum term of the contract:** The selected subscription period (one month or one year). The subscription continues as long as the Buyer renews it.
- **Requirements:** An up-to-date web browser, an internet connection and, to send messages, the Buyer's own Meta Business and WhatsApp Business account.

## 4. Total price

- **The total price including VAT (KDV) shown in the order summary** is the amount to be paid by the Buyer. The plan price, VAT and total are shown separately.
- There are no delivery or shipping costs.
- **Meta fees are not included in the price;** the fees charged by Meta for WhatsApp messages are collected directly by Meta from the Buyer's Meta account. Before a campaign is sent, the Service shows the estimated Meta cost.

## 5. Payment, performance and delivery

- **Payment:** By bank transfer or EFT to the bank account stated in the order summary, entering the reference number in the description. Card payment coming soon (PayTR).
- **Performance:** The Service is not delivered physically. For bank transfer and EFT payments, the plan is activated in the account within 1 business day at the latest after the transfer is credited to the Seller's account and matched. Once card payment is introduced, activation for card payments takes place instantly. Details: [Service Delivery and Performance](/en/legal/service-delivery).
- **Invoice:** Sent by e-mail as an e-Fatura (e-invoice) or e-Arşiv (e-archive) invoice.

## 6. Right of withdrawal

- A Buyer who is a consumer has the right of withdrawal within **14 days** from the day the contract is concluded, without giving any reason and without paying any penalty.
- **Exception:** Under the first paragraph of Article 15 of the Distance Contracts Regulation, the right of withdrawal cannot be exercised for services performed instantly in electronic form and for services whose performance has started with the consumer's approval before the withdrawal period expires. If you expressly approve during ordering that the service should start immediately, you lose your right of withdrawal. If you do not give this approval, your right of withdrawal continues.
- **Withdrawal notice:** In writing or by a durable medium to {{eposta}} or to {{adres}}; also via the [Contact](/en/contact) form.
- **Refund:** Within 14 days of the withdrawal notice being received, using the method by which payment was made and free of charge.
- Full cancellation and refund conditions: [Cancellation, Refund and Withdrawal Terms](/en/legal/cancellation-and-refunds).

## 7. Information on digital content

- The Service runs in the browser; no installation is required.
- For account security, a password, optional two-factor authentication (TOTP) and role-based authorisation are used. Apart from these, there are no technical protection measures restricting the functionality of the Service; use is limited by plan limits.
- Message sending depends on the availability of Meta's WhatsApp Business Platform and the status of the Buyer's Meta account.

## 8. Complaints and dispute resolution

- Complaints can be sent via {{eposta}}, {{telefon}}, {{kep}} or {{adres}}.
- A Buyer who is a consumer may apply, within the monetary limits announced by the Ministry of Trade, to the Consumer Arbitration Committee (Tüketici Hakem Heyeti), and, for disputes exceeding those limits, to the Consumer Court (Tüketici Mahkemesi).
- If the Buyer is a merchant, the Law does not apply; the [Terms of Service](/en/legal/terms-of-service) apply and the courts and enforcement offices of {{sehir}} have jurisdiction.
