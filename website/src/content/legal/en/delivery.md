{{marka}} is a software service (SaaS) provided over the internet by {{unvan}}. This page explains how and when the service you purchase is made available to you.

This English version is provided for convenience; the Turkish version prevails in case of any discrepancy.

## 1. There is no physical delivery

No physical product, shipment or installation media is sent for the {{marka}} service. The Service is performed electronically and used through the browser. No delivery or shipping fee is charged.

## 2. Opening the account and the trial period

- Registration is done at [app.messora.tech/signup](https://app.messora.tech/signup).
- Once your e-mail address is verified, your account is opened **immediately** and the 14-day free trial begins. No card details are required.
- You access your account through the shared panel at `app.messora.tech`. On the Enterprise plan you get your own subdomain in the form `firma.messora.tech` and can connect your own domain; in that case the TLS certificate is issued automatically. Configuring the DNS settings of your own domain is your responsibility, and the propagation of DNS changes depends on your domain provider.

## 3. Activating a paid plan

| Payment method | Activation |
|---|---|
| Bank transfer / EFT | **Within 1 business day at the latest** after the transfer is credited to our account and matched |
| Credit / debit card (coming soon, PayTR) | Once card payment is introduced, as soon as the payment is approved |
| Enterprise plan | On the date set in the written agreement |

Entering the reference number from the order summary in the description field when making a bank transfer or EFT speeds up matching. Matching may be delayed for payments without a reference or with an insufficient amount; in that case we will contact you. The time it takes for interbank EFT transactions to reach our account depends on the banks' working hours.

When the plan is activated, you are notified by e-mail and the invoice is sent as an e-Fatura (e-invoice) or e-Arşiv (e-archive) invoice. Your data and settings from the trial period are carried over to the paid plan unchanged.

## 4. Processes that depend on Meta

To send messages, you need to connect your own Meta Business and WhatsApp Business account to {{marka}}. The connection is made using your own Meta app (App ID and System User token), following the method explained step by step in the panel. One-click connection (Embedded Signup) will be offered soon.

The following processes **depend on Meta and are outside {{marka}}'s control:**

- Meta business verification (Business Verification),
- registration of the telephone number and approval of the display name,
- review and approval of template messages,
- messaging limits (tiers) and quality rating,
- collection of Meta fees.

The time these processes take is determined by Meta. Delays in Meta's processes do not mean that the {{marka}} service has not been performed; we guide you through these processes via the panel and our support channels. Meta's official documentation: [developers.facebook.com/docs/whatsapp](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/whatsapp).

## 5. Continuity of the Service

We aim to provide the Service without interruption throughout your subscription period. Planned maintenance is announced in advance wherever possible. Provisions on the service level are set out in the [Terms of Service](/en/legal/terms-of-service).
