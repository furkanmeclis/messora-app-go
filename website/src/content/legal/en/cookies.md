## 1. What is a cookie

Cookies are small text files saved on your device through your browser when you visit a website. Cookies are used for purposes such as making the site work, remembering your preferences, measuring how the site is used or evaluating the effectiveness of advertisements. In this policy, the term "cookie" also covers similar technologies such as browser storage.

A cookie is a **first-party** cookie if it is placed by the site itself, and a **third-party** cookie if it is placed by another company. Cookies deleted when the browser is closed are called **session cookies**, and those stored for a certain period are called **persistent cookies**.

## 2. Cookie categories we use

### 2.1. Essential cookies

These are necessary for the website to work securely and properly; they cannot be switched off. These cookies record your cookie preference and protect forms against misuse. When a form is submitted, a technical security value valid only for the duration of the session may be used to block automated submissions and forged requests. Your explicit consent is not required for essential cookies; their basis is necessity for providing the service and our legitimate interest (KVKK Art. 5/2-f).

### 2.2. Analytics cookies

These help us measure, in aggregate, how the website is used (such as pages visited, session duration and referring source) and improve the site. Google Analytics 4 is used for this purpose. **Analytics cookies run only if you give explicit consent.**

### 2.3. Marketing and advertising cookies

These are used to measure conversions on the website resulting from our Google Ads advertisements (for example, a form being filled in). **Marketing cookies run only if you give explicit consent.**

## 3. Google Consent Mode (Consent Mode v2)

Our website uses Google tags (gtag) with version two of Google Consent Mode. When you first open the site, analytics and advertising storage permissions are **denied** by default (`analytics_storage`, `ad_storage`, `ad_user_data`, `ad_personalization`). In that state, Google cookies are not written to your device. When you consent to the analytics or marketing category, only the permissions belonging to the category you consented to are granted. When you withdraw your consent, the permissions are switched off again and no new Google cookie is placed.

## 5. How to change your preferences

In the cookie notice that opens on your first visit to the site, you can accept all cookies, reject non-essential cookies or select categories individually. The "Reject" option is as easy to access as the "Accept" option; rejecting does not prevent you from using the site.

You can change your preferences at any time: the "Cookie preferences" button on this page and at the bottom of the site (footer) opens the preference centre. There you can withdraw the consent you gave or consent to a new category. Withdrawing your consent does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out up to the date of withdrawal.

## 6. Browser settings

You can also manage, delete or block cookies through your browser settings. In common browsers, these settings are usually found in the "Privacy", "Privacy and security" or "Site data" sections. If you block all cookies, your cookie preference cannot be remembered, so the notice may be shown again on each visit and some functions of the site may not work as expected. You can also opt out of Google Analytics measurement using the browser add-on offered by Google.

## 7. Legal ground

- **Essential cookies:** KVKK Art. 5/2-f (legitimate interest); necessary for providing the site securely and remembering your preferences.
- **Analytics and marketing cookies:** your explicit consent under KVKK Art. 5/1. Explicit consent is given on a specific matter, based on information and with free will. For this reason, these cookies are off by default, no pre-ticked boxes are used and continuing to use the site is not deemed consent.
