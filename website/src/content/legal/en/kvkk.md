## 3. Data subject groups and personal data processed

The tables below show, for each data subject group, the categories of data we process, the purposes for which we process them and the legal grounds on which we rely. Legal grounds are cited by reference to the sub-paragraphs of the second paragraph of Article 5 of the KVKK.

**Data processed:** Transaction security data (IP address, browser and device information, date and time of visit, request logs), the record of your cookie preference; and, only if you give consent, usage data obtained through analytics and marketing cookies (pages visited, session duration, approximate location, referring source, ad click information).

| Measuring and improving website usage (Google Analytics) | Explicit consent (Art. 5/1) |

## 6. Retention periods

Personal data are retained for as long as necessary for the purpose for which they are processed and for the period provided in the relevant legislation; when that period expires, they are deleted, destroyed or anonymised in accordance with the Regulation on the Deletion, Destruction or Anonymisation of Personal Data (Kişisel Verilerin Silinmesi, Yok Edilmesi veya Anonim Hale Getirilmesi Hakkında Yönetmelik).

| Data group | Retention period |
|---|---|
| Contract, subscription, order and correspondence records | 10 years from the end of the contract (Turkish Code of Obligations No. 6098 (TBK) Art. 146; TTK No. 6102 Art. 82) |
| Invoice, payment and accounting records | 10 years from the end of the relevant calendar year (TTK Art. 82; at least 5 years under VUK No. 213 Art. 253) |
| Panel account information (User name, e-mail, role) | While the account is active; after the account or contract ends, until the end of the data export period of at most 30 days, after which it is deleted. It may remain in backups for up to a further 35 days. The part linked to contract and invoice records is retained for the periods above. |
| Audit log and security logs | The period set by the Customer; if not set, 2 years from the date of the record |
| Website traffic data and server logs | At least 1 and at most 2 years under Law No. 5651 and the related regulation |
| Contact and demo/quote form records (not converted into a contract) | 2 years from the date of last contact |
| Support requests | 3 years from closure of the request; if related to a dispute, until the dispute is resolved |
| Commercial electronic message consent and opt-out records | 3 years from the date the consent ceases to be valid |
| KVKK requests and responses | 3 years from the conclusion of the request |
| Cookie data | The period stated for each cookie in the [Cookie Policy](/en/legal/cookie-policy) |
| Supplier and business partner records | 10 years from the end of the relationship |

Where there is a dispute, investigation or request from a competent authority, the relevant data may be retained for as long as those proceedings require.

## 7. Rights of the data subject (KVKK Art. 11)

Under Article 11 of the KVKK, by applying to the Company you have the right to:

1. learn whether your personal data are processed,
2. request information about the processing if your personal data have been processed,
3. learn the purpose of processing of your personal data and whether they are used in accordance with that purpose,
4. know the third parties in Türkiye or abroad to whom your personal data are transferred,
5. request the rectification of your personal data if they are incomplete or inaccurate,
6. request the deletion or destruction of your personal data under the conditions set out in Article 7 of the KVKK,
7. request that the operations carried out under sub-paragraphs 5 and 6 be notified to the third parties to whom your personal data have been transferred,
8. object to a result to your detriment arising from the analysis of the processed data exclusively through automated systems,
9. claim compensation for damage if you suffer damage due to the unlawful processing of your personal data.

For processing based on explicit consent, you may withdraw your consent at any time; withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out up to that date.

If you are a customer of a {{marka}} Customer (for example, if you received a WhatsApp message from a business), the data controller for your data is the relevant business. We recommend that you submit your request directly to that business. If your request reaches us, we will, to the extent permitted by law, forward it to the relevant Customer and support the Customer in responding.

## 8. How to submit a request

Under the Communiqué on the Procedures and Principles of Application to the Data Controller (Veri Sorumlusuna Başvuru Usul ve Esasları Hakkında Tebliğ), you may submit your request in Turkish by one of the following methods:

- **In writing:** by a petition bearing your wet signature, delivered in person or through a notary public to {{adres}}, or by registered mail with return receipt;
- **By KEP (registered electronic mail):** to {{kep}};
- **With a secure electronic signature or mobile signature:** to {{kvkkEposta}};
- **From your e-mail address registered in our system:** from the e-mail address you previously notified to the Company and that is registered in our system, to {{kvkkEposta}};
- **Using the request form:** through the online request form at {{basvuruUrl}}.

Your request must include your first name and last name and, if the request is in writing, your signature; for citizens of the Republic of Türkiye, your Turkish national identity number; for foreign nationals, your nationality and passport number or, if any, identity number; your residential or business address for notifications; if any, your e-mail address for notifications; your telephone number; and the subject of your request. Information and documents relating to your request should be attached. We may ask for additional information to verify your identity; this is to prevent your data from being disclosed to someone else.

Your request will be concluded free of charge as soon as possible depending on its nature and **within thirty (30) days at the latest**. However, if the operation requires an additional cost, a fee may be charged according to the tariff set by the Personal Data Protection Board (Kurul); under the Communiqué, if the response is given in writing and exceeds ten pages, a fee may be charged for each page, and if the response is given on a recording medium such as a CD or flash drive, a fee up to the cost of the recording medium may be charged. If the request arises from an error by the Company, any fee charged will be refunded.

If your request is rejected, the response is found insufficient or no response is given in time, you may file a complaint with the Personal Data Protection Board within thirty days from the date you learn of the response and, in any case, within sixty days from the date of your request ([kvkk.gov.tr](https://www.kvkk.gov.tr)).
