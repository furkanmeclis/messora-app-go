This page explains the cancellation, refund and withdrawal terms of the {{marka}} software service provided by {{unvan}} ("**{{marka}}**"). These terms form an integral part of the [Terms of Service](/en/legal/terms-of-service) and the [Distance Sales Agreement](/en/legal/distance-sales-agreement). For buyers who are consumers, the mandatory provisions of Law No. 6502 on Consumer Protection (Tüketicinin Korunması Hakkında Kanun) and the related regulations are reserved in all cases.

This English version is provided for convenience; the Turkish version prevails in case of any discrepancy.

## 1. Free trial period

The 14-day trial period is free and no card details are required. You can close your account at any time during the trial period; no fee is incurred. If you do not select a paid plan at the end of the trial period, you are not charged.

## 2. Monthly plans

- You can cancel your monthly plan at any time through the panel or by notifying {{eposta}}.
- Cancellation takes effect at the **end** of the paid monthly period; you continue to use the Service until the end of the period.
- No partial (pro rata) refund is made for the current month.

## 3. Yearly plans

- You can cancel your yearly plan at any time; cancellation takes effect at the end of the paid yearly period and you continue to use the Service until the end of the period.
- For yearly plans, **no** proportional refund is made for unused months. The discount applied to yearly payment is based on this principle.
- In the following cases, the price for the unused period is refunded proportionally:
  - {{marka}} discontinuing the Service entirely;
  - you terminating the contract due to a material breach of contract by {{marka}};
  - you ending your subscription before the effective date because you do not accept changes to the [Terms of Service](/en/legal/terms-of-service), or a force majeure event lasting more than 30 days;
  - other cases where the legislation requires a refund.

## 4. Right of withdrawal (for consumers)

- Buyers who are consumers have the right of withdrawal within 14 days from the day the service contract is concluded, without giving any reason.
- Under the first paragraph of Article 15 of the Distance Contracts Regulation (Mesafeli Sözleşmeler Yönetmeliği), the right of withdrawal cannot be exercised for **services performed instantly in electronic form** and **services whose performance has started with the consumer's approval before the withdrawal period expires**. If you expressly approve, using a separate checkbox during ordering, that the service should start immediately, your right of withdrawal ends.
- For orders where you did not give this approval, you can exercise your right of withdrawal; the price is refunded within 14 days of your withdrawal notice reaching us.
- Merchants and legal entities purchasing for commercial or professional purposes are not deemed consumers; the right of withdrawal does not apply to these buyers.

## 5. Add-on credits and balances

If offered in the future, for AI credit packages, message balances or similar prepaid usage rights purchased in addition to a plan:

- The used portion is not refunded.
- The unused portion is refunded only if {{marka}} discontinues the Service.
- Monthly AI credits and API quotas granted each month with the plan do not roll over to the following month and cannot be exchanged for cash.

## 6. Meta fees

The fees charged by Meta for WhatsApp messages are collected by Meta directly from your Meta account. As these fees are not collected by {{marka}}, they cannot be refunded by {{marka}}. Objections relating to Meta fees are made through Meta's own processes. Where it has been separately agreed under the Enterprise plan that Meta fees are passed through {{marka}}, amounts not refunded by Meta are not refunded by {{marka}} either.

## 7. Suspension and termination by {{marka}}

If the account is suspended or the contract is terminated because of use contrary to the [Terms of Service](/en/legal/terms-of-service), Meta policies or the legislation, the price paid is not refunded. Mandatory provisions concerning consumers are reserved.

## 8. Refund method and period

- Refunds are made through the channel used for payment: payments made by bank transfer or EFT are refunded **to the account from which payment was sent**.
- Once card payment is introduced, refunds of card payments are made through PayTR to the card used for payment. The time it takes for the amount to appear in your account depends on your bank.
- The refund is made **within 14 days at the latest** from the approval of the request (in the case of withdrawal, from your withdrawal notice reaching us).
- Documents required for the refund (such as a return invoice or expense voucher (gider pusulası)) are issued in accordance with the legislation; merchant buyers may be asked to issue a return invoice.

## 9. How to request a cancellation or refund

- **Through the panel:** You can cancel your subscription in the billing section of the panel.
- **By e-mail:** To {{eposta}}, stating your company name in the panel (or, on Enterprise, your own address such as `firma.messora.tech`), your legal name or name, your order reference and your request.
- **Using the contact form:** Through the form on the [Contact](/en/contact) page.
- **By KEP (registered electronic mail):** To {{kep}}.

When your request is received, you are sent a confirmation, and your request is concluded within 15 days at the latest.

## 10. Your data

You can export your data for 30 days after your subscription ends. At the end of this period the data are deleted; encrypted backups are renewed on a cycle of at most 35 days. Details are set out in the [Terms of Service](/en/legal/terms-of-service) and the [Data Processing Agreement](/en/legal/data-processing-agreement).
