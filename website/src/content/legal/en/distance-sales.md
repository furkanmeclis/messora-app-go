## 2. Subject of the Agreement

The subject of the Agreement is the determination, in accordance with the provisions of the Law and the Regulation, of the parties' rights and obligations regarding the Buyer's electronic purchase of the subscription plan stated in the order summary for the Seller's {{marka}} software service (the "**Service**").

## 4. Price and payment

4.1. The price of the Service is **the total price including VAT (KDV) shown in the order summary.** The order summary shows the plan price, the VAT amount and the total amount separately.

4.2. No separate delivery or shipping fee is charged for providing the Service.

4.3. **Meta fees are not included in the price.** The fees charged by Meta for messages sent through WhatsApp are collected directly by Meta through the payment method registered to the Buyer's Meta account.

4.4. Payment is made by **bank transfer or EFT** to the bank account stated in the order summary, entering the reference number from the order summary in the description. Card payment will soon be offered through PayTR; card details are not stored by the Seller.

4.5. After payment is received, the invoice is issued as an e-Fatura (e-invoice) or e-Arşiv (e-archive) invoice and sent to the Buyer's e-mail address.

## 5. Performance of the Service

5.1. The Service is not delivered physically; it is performed electronically.

5.2. For payments made by bank transfer or EFT, the plan is activated in the Buyer's account **within 1 business day at the latest after the transfer is credited to the Seller's account and matched**. Once card payment is introduced, activation for approved card payments takes place instantly.

5.3. Details are set out on the [Service Delivery and Performance](/en/legal/service-delivery) page. Meta business verification, number connection and template approval processes depend on Meta and are outside the Seller's control.

## 6. Right of withdrawal

6.1. If the Buyer is a consumer within the scope of the Law, for service contracts the Buyer has the right to withdraw from the contract **within 14 days from the day the contract is concluded**, without giving any reason and without paying any penalty.

6.2. **Exception to the right of withdrawal:** Under the first paragraph of Article 15 of the Regulation, the right of withdrawal cannot be exercised for contracts relating to **services performed instantly in electronic form** and **services whose performance has started with the consumer's approval before the withdrawal period expires**. The Service is activated electronically once payment is approved. During ordering, by ticking a separate checkbox, the Buyer expressly confirms that it requests performance of the service to begin within the withdrawal period and that it knows it will therefore lose the right of withdrawal. For orders placed without giving this approval, the right of withdrawal applies in accordance with clause 6.1.

6.3. Where the right of withdrawal can be exercised, the Buyer sends the withdrawal notice within the period in writing or by a durable medium to {{eposta}} or to {{adres}}. It is sufficient for the notice to state the name or legal name, the account address and the order reference. The Buyer may also use the [Contact](/en/contact) form.

6.4. Within **14 days** of the date the withdrawal notice reaches it, the Seller refunds the amount collected using the method by which payment was made (for payments by bank transfer, to the account from which payment was made) and without charging the Buyer any cost.

6.5. The free trial period is not a sale; no fee is paid during the trial period and the Buyer may close its account at any time.

## 7. Ending the subscription

The Buyer may end its subscription at any time through the panel or by notifying {{eposta}}. Termination takes effect at the end of the paid period. For Buyers who are consumers, Article 51 of the Law and the mandatory provisions of the relevant legislation on subscription contracts are reserved. Refund conditions are set out in the [Cancellation, Refund and Withdrawal Terms](/en/legal/cancellation-and-refunds).

## 8. Obligations of the parties

8.1. The Seller is obliged to provide the Service in accordance with the characteristics stated in the order summary and in the [Terms of Service](/en/legal/terms-of-service).

8.2. The Buyer is obliged to use the Service in accordance with the [Terms of Service](/en/legal/terms-of-service), Meta and WhatsApp policies and the legislation, and to pay the price on time.

## 9. Complaints and objections

9.1. The Buyer may send complaints and requests regarding the Service to {{eposta}}, to the telephone number {{telefon}}, to the KEP address {{kep}} or to {{adres}}. Complaints are answered within 15 days at the latest.

9.2. In the event of a dispute, a Buyer who is a consumer may apply, within the monetary limits announced annually by the Ministry of Trade, to the **Consumer Arbitration Committee (Tüketici Hakem Heyeti)** at its place of residence or where the consumer transaction was carried out, and, for disputes exceeding those limits, to the **Consumer Court (Tüketici Mahkemesi)**. Applications may also be made through the consumer application system offered by the Ministry of Trade via e-Devlet (e-Government) ([ticaret.gov.tr](https://www.ticaret.gov.tr)).

## 10. Where the Buyer is a merchant

If the Buyer is a merchant or legal entity purchasing the Service for commercial or professional purposes, the Buyer is not deemed a consumer; in that case the rights granted to consumers by the Law and the Regulation, in particular the right of withdrawal, do not apply, and the [Terms of Service](/en/legal/terms-of-service) and general provisions apply to the relationship between the parties. The courts and enforcement offices of {{sehir}} have jurisdiction over disputes.
